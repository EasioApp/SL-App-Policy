Privacy Policy for Shift Ledger

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Shift Ledger is a local-first application designed with user privacy as its core foundational principle. Your financial and employment data belongs exclusively to you, remains stored on your device, and is never sold, transmitted to our servers, or shared with third parties for advertising.

1. Introduction & Data Controller Information

This Privacy Policy explains how Shift Ledger ("the App", "we", "us", "our") processes information when you use our mobile application.

For the purposes of applicable data protection legislationincluding the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 (KVKK)the Data Controller (Veri Sorumlusu) responsible for the limited technical and analytical data processed by the App is:

Developer / Trade Name: Easio App

Contact Email: easio.app@gmail.com

2. The Core Architecture: Local-First Data Storage

The vast majority of data you input into Shift Ledgerincluding employer details, shift schedules, hourly/monthly wage rates, financial income and deduction entries, notes, and ledger running balancesis stored exclusively on your personal device within an encrypted local database (SQLCipher).

     No Central Server: We do not operate, own, or maintain a remote server that receives, collects, or stores your financial or employment records.

     No Access or Recovery: We cannot view, access, monitor, or recover your personal financial records. If you lose your device without creating a backup, we cannot restore your data.

3. Information We Process and Collection Purposes

While your personal ledger remains strictly on your device, limited non-financial information is processed through localized or third-party infrastructure to facilitate core features:

3.1 Subscription & Purchase Data

     Data Processed: Subscription status (e.g., Free vs. Pro entitlement, entitlement expiration date / proValidUntil), purchase token, and an anonymized device/account identifier.

     Payment Processing: All financial transactions are handled directly by Google Play Billing. We never receive, process, or store your credit card or banking details.

     Provider Used: We use RevenueCat to verify and synchronize subscription entitlements.

     Legal Basis: Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) / KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).

3.2 On-Device App Integrity Checks

Upon cold startup, the App locally verifies its installation signature against official release signatures to prevent execution of tampered or counterfeit binaries. This process runs entirely offline on your device without transmitting telemetry.

4. Optional Cloud Backups and Passwords

Cloud backup is completely optional and disabled by default.

4.1 Backup Destination

If you enable cloud backup, your encrypted backup package (.bak) is uploaded directly to your personal Google Drive account. We do not host, store, or have access to your Google Drive storage or backup files.

4.2 Backup Encryption Passwords

You may optionally encrypt your backup file using a custom password:

     Zero-Knowledge Architecture: Backup passwords are derived locally on your device (using Argon2id / PBKDF2 key derivation) and are never transmitted to or stored on our systems.

     Your Responsibility: If you set a backup password and forget it, we cannot reset or recover it, and your backup file cannot be decrypted.

     Unencrypted Backups: If you opt out of password protection, your backup file relies entirely on the inherent security settings of your personal Google account.

5. Third-Party Service Providers & Cross-Border Data Transfers

The App relies on trusted third-party SDKs to provide payment processing, subscription management, and cloud storage integrations. These services may process anonymized or account-level data on servers located outside your country of residence (including outside the EU/EEA or Türkiye):

     Google Play Services & Google Drive API (see Google Privacy Policy)  Subscription payments and optional personal drive backup storage.

     RevenueCat Inc. (see RevenueCat Privacy Policy)  Subscription entitlement verification.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

6. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

     Local Data Retention: Your financial entries and employer logs are preserved indefinitely using local soft-delete mechanisms. We never automatically purge, expire, or delete your financial data over time.

     Permanent Deletion: You retain complete authority over your data. You may permanently delete your data at any time by executing a full data wipe via Settings → Data Management → Clear All Data (which requires double confirmation), or by uninstalling the App (if no external backup exists).

7. Data Security

     Database Encryption: Local application data is protected using full-database encryption via SQLCipher.

     Token Security: Authentication tokens (such as Google OAuth tokens) are secured via the native Android Keystore system.

     Financial Screen Protection: In accordance with your plan level, sensitive financial interfaces apply device-level window protection (FLAG_SECURE) to prevent unauthorized local screenshots, screen recordings, or recent-app switcher previews.

     Right to learn whether your personal data is processed,

     Right to request information if your personal data has been processed,

     Right to learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used in accordance with its purpose,

     Right to know third parties to whom data is transferred domestically or internationally,

     Right to request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data,

     Right to request deletion or destruction of data pursuant to Article 7 of KVKK,

     Right to object to any adverse result arising from automated analysis of data.

To exercise these rights regarding subscription or analytics logs, contact us at easio.app@gmail.com. Note that for data stored locally on your device or in your personal Google Drive, you exercise these rights directly through the App interface.

9. Rights under GDPR (EU/UK Users)

Under the GDPR, users in the EU/UK have rights to access, rectify, port, or erase the limited technical/subscription data we maintain, or to restrict/object to its processing. Since local ledger records do not exist on our servers, data access and erasure for financial logs are performed directly by you inside the App.

10. Children's Privacy

Shift Ledger is not directed to individuals under the age of 13, or the applicable higher minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction (for example, up to 16 in parts of the EU/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
